# Maps (`10-maps`)

## Purpose

Maps provide curated paths through related research without duplicating the
archive's exhaustive directory indexes.

## What belongs here

Put selective topic maps, question maps, and the archive home map here. A map
should explain why its links belong together rather than merely list files.
